What This Is

A Collector's App, Not a Marketplace

Zetsu Packz is a pre-launch collecting experience for the Zetsumetsu trading card game. It's a place to open packs, build a personal collection, and watch a card's standing shift as the whole community collects around it.

It works more like a card shop than a vending machine — you're not trading a commodity for cash, you're building something and getting to know the cards before the full TCG releases.

How It Works

Buy. Rip. Collect.

1

Buy a Tier

Pick a rarity tier in the Card Shop — Common through Mythic. The price you pay is that tier's value. No hidden odds on rarity — buying a tier guarantees it.

2

Rip It Open

Head to Rip Pack to reveal which specific card design you pulled from within that tier.

3

Keep It or Redeem It

Hold the card in your Collection, or redeem it for its current tier value, added straight to your Redemption Balance.

The Popularity Ledger

The Community Decides What's Rare

Every card starts at Common.

Every card design begins life worth $5 — except a small handful of preset Mythics seeded into the deck at launch.

From there, a card's tier isn't fixed forever. Once a quarter, the Popularity Ledger reviews how much each card has actually been bought across the whole community. Cards that get bought enough ascend to the next tier up. Cards that cool off can descend back down. Common is the floor, Mythic is the ceiling — nothing falls below or climbs past either end.

That means the cards worth the most aren't the ones you were told were rare — they're the ones the whole community made rare, together.

Redemption

Turning a Collection Into Something Real

Redeeming a card adds its current tier value to your Redemption Balance. Once that balance builds up enough, it converts into a real gift card.

Why Now

Get to Know the Cards Before the Game Drops

Zetsu Packz exists to let people meet the Zetsumetsu card roster, build history with it, and shape which designs rise to the top — all before the full TCG launches. By the time the real game arrives, the community will have already decided what's legendary.
