Ascension Threshold

150% of tier average, annual basis. Same thresholds apply in reverse for descension — a card drops a tier if its trailing popularity falls under the threshold for the tier below its current one.

Current State — Core Rules

Buy Price to Buy = tier's dollar value ($5 / $10 / $25 / $100 / $150)
Redeem Pays out at current tier value, in chips — card stays in circulation, isn't destroyed
Starting value Every card design starts at Common ($5) except a handful of preset Mythics — $555 deck at launch
Ascend / Descend Quarterly, based on net buys vs. threshold (150% of tier average)
Floor / Ceiling Common can't drop further, Mythic can't climb further
Cash out 150 chips → $100 gift card (50-chip service fee is the house margin)
Pack sell-back Doesn't exist — only opened / redeemed cards can be sold
