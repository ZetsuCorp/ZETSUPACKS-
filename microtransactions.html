Other ways to spend chips beyond buying and redeeming cards
Gathering Parts — Not Yet in Production
Buy cards → sell cards → spend chips to morph cards
More reasons for chips to keep moving instead of draining one direction. Less likely to leave the app, more app and TCG growth.

Chip-Spend Ideas

Gathering stage — no pricing locked yet on most of these. Nothing here is in production.

Fusion
Paid · Chips
Spend chips to morph a card's finish — Normal → Holographic → Holo Foil. Changes look only, never tier or redemption value. Tier and finish are fully separate axes, so this can never touch the locked economics.
Chip cost: not yet priced
Kickstart the Next Deck
Paid · Chips
Chips go directly toward funding the next deck's release. Makes the "fee funds new decks" framing interactive and visible instead of just copy on the Redemption page.
Ties to real deck cost: $555+ per deck
Raffles
Paid · Chips
Main pull-back mechanic. Chips buy raffle entries, prizes are cards and/or cosmetics. This is what keeps chips cycling back into the economy instead of only draining outward.
Entry cost / prize pool: not yet priced
Sleeve / Frame
Paid · Chips
Cosmetic border/sleeve upgrade for a card. Pure flex spend, no effect on tier or value.
Chip cost: not yet priced
Showcase
Paid · Chips
Featured display slot for a player's best pulls on their profile.
Chip cost: not yet priced
Share the App → Get a Pack
Free · Growth
Sharing the app earns a free pack. Not a chip sink — this is a growth/acquisition lever, not a spend.
Reward: 1 free pack
