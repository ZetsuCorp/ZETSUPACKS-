What the game is, and how the account works
Terms of Service

What This App Is

Zetsu Packz is a TCG collector's app, released ahead of the full launch of the Zetsumetsu trading card game. It's a place to collect cards, build a personal collection, and watch card tiers move with community activity through the Popularity Ledger.

Zetsu Packz is not a marketplace, a buy-and-sell trading platform, or a gambling product. It works more like a card shop than a vending machine — you're building a collection, not trading a commodity for cash.

Chips

Chips are Zetsu Packz's in-app currency. Chips have no monetary value outside the app and cannot be sold, transferred, or exchanged for cash directly. Chips are used to acquire cards and, separately, to accumulate Redemption Balance.

Cards & Tiers

Every card belongs to a rarity tier (Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic). A card's tier can rise or fall over time based on community-wide activity, reviewed on a quarterly basis through the Popularity Ledger. Tier standing is program-determined, not set by an open market between users.

Redemption Balance & Gift Cards

Redeeming a card adds its current tier value to your Redemption Balance. Once your balance reaches the stated threshold, you may request a gift card.

Requesting a gift card is not an automatic, instant payout. Zetsu Packz does not guarantee that a gift card reward will be available at any given moment for any given user, and reserves the right to modify, pause, or adjust the redemption program — including thresholds, payout amounts, or the service fee — at any time.

A portion of every gift card conversion is kept as a service fee. This fee funds new card decks, community operations, and the ongoing maintenance of the Popularity Ledger — it is not treated as unrestricted profit sitting idle.

No Guarantee of Value

Card tiers, chip amounts, and redemption rates reflect current program settings, not a resale market. Past tier standing does not guarantee future tier standing. Nothing in Zetsu Packz should be treated as an investment or a financial asset.

Right to Modify

Zetsu Packz is in active development ahead of the full TCG launch. Features, pricing, tiers, chip rates, and redemption terms may change as the app evolves.

Eligibility

You must meet the applicable minimum age in your region to make purchases or request gift card redemption.

Account & Fair Use

Zetsu Packz reserves the right to suspend or limit accounts found to be abusing the app, its Popularity Ledger, or its redemption program.

Privacy Policy

What We Collect

  • Account information — whatever's needed to identify your account and hold your chip balance, collection, and Redemption Balance.
  • Email address — used specifically to fulfill gift card redemption requests.
  • Usage data — purchases, redemptions, and collection activity, used to run the Popularity Ledger.

How We Use It

Your data is used to operate your account, fulfill gift card redemption requests, run the quarterly Popularity Ledger, and improve the app.

What We Don't Do

Zetsu Packz does not sell personal data to third parties. Information is only shared where necessary to fulfill a redemption — for example, with a gift card provider.

Children's Privacy

Zetsu Packz is not directed at children under the applicable minimum age, and does not knowingly collect data from users below that age.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the Zetsu Packz team through the app.
